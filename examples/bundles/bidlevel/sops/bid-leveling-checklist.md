# SOP: bid leveling checklist

1. Read the trade package's scope of work and the instructions to bidders.
2. Map each bid's line items to the scope; note anything missing or extra.
3. List every stated exclusion and inclusion side by side across bidders.
4. Normalize unit prices and alternates so totals compare apples to apples.
5. Ask the estimator about anything ambiguous (allowances, escalation terms).
6. Hold the leveling sheet as a draft until a human reviews it.
